PART 1

VPN BASICS — FOR EVERYONE

WHAT IS A VPN?

A VPN (virtual private network) creates an encrypted tunnel between your device and a server run by the VPN provider. Normally, a website sees your IP address directly. With a VPN, it sees the VPN server's IP instead of yours.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Normally: You → Website (the website sees your IP)
With a VPN: You → VPN Server → Website (the website sees the VPN's IP, not yours)

What a VPN Does NOT Do

  • Your VPN provider can see your activity. You're shifting trust from your ISP to your VPN provider — choose wisely (see Part 2).
  • A VPN does not make you anonymous. It makes you private from your ISP, but not from the VPN provider.
LEGAL NOTE

VPNs are legal in most countries but restricted in Iran, Russia, China, and Turkey. Check your local laws.

PART 2

CHOOSING A VPN PROVIDER — FOR EVERYONE

Trustworthy Signals
  • Independent, third-party security audit published within the last year
  • No-logs policy proven in court or by a real seizure incident, not just claimed
  • Open-source apps and/or published transparency reports
  • Accepts privacy-preserving payment (cash, Monero) as an option
  • Runs its own infrastructure rather than leasing servers it doesn't fully control

Corporate Ownership Webs

Many "independent-looking" VPN brands are owned by the same handful of parent holding companies, which also run VPN review sites that rank their own products highly. Before trusting a glowing "best VPN" list, check who owns both the VPN and the site reviewing it.

Marketing Red Flags
  • "Military-grade encryption" — a meaningless marketing phrase, not a real spec
  • Claims of "complete anonymity" — no VPN can honestly promise this
  • Aggressive affiliate-driven discounts and countdown timers pushing an annual plan before you can compare
  • No published audit, but a large "trusted by millions" banner
PART 3 — BEGINNER

USING A VPN

Quick Setup (App-Based)

  1. Sign up with your chosen provider and download the official app for your device.
  2. Install it and log in (or paste your anonymous account number, if the provider supports one).
  3. Pick a nearby server for speed, or a specific country if you need that location.
  4. Enable the "kill switch" setting, which blocks all traffic if the VPN connection drops.
  5. Turn on "connect automatically" so it activates whenever your device starts.

PART 4 — ADVANCED

MANUAL VPN SETUP

Prerequisites

  • A WireGuard-compatible configuration file or QR code from your VPN provider
  • A router firmware (e.g. OpenWrt, pfSense) or OS (Linux, macOS, Windows) with WireGuard support
  • Comfort using a terminal or your router's admin panel
  • A backup way online (e.g. mobile data) in case the setup locks you out of your router

Step-by-Step Lab

  1. Install the WireGuard tools/package for your OS or router firmware.
  2. Import the configuration file from your provider (or generate a keypair and register the public key in your provider's dashboard).
  3. Bring the interface up (e.g. wg-quick up wg0 on Linux) and confirm a handshake completes.
  4. Set the tunnel's DNS to your provider's DNS servers, not your ISP's.
  5. Add a firewall rule that drops all traffic if the tunnel goes down (a manual kill switch).

Browser Leak Fix

WebRTC can leak your real IP even through a working tunnel. In Firefox, set media.peerconnection.enabled to false in about:config if you don't need video calling; in Chromium-based browsers, use an extension like WebRTC Leak Prevent, or rely on Brave's built-in WebRTC policy control in Shields.

IPv6 Fix

Many VPN tunnels only route IPv4 traffic. If your network has IPv6 enabled, your real IPv6 address can leak outside the tunnel entirely. Either disable IPv6 on the interface you're tunneling, or confirm your provider explicitly tunnels IPv6 too, then retest at ipleak.net.

Certificate Maintenance

Rotate your WireGuard keypair periodically (every 6–12 months) and immediately if a device holding the private key is lost or compromised. Remove old public keys from your provider's dashboard so a stolen config file stops working.
