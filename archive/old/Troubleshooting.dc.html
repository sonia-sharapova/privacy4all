FROM: SET UP YOUR BROWSER

WHEN SITES BREAK

PROBLEM
LIKELY CAUSE
SOLUTION
Video won't play
Ad blocker blocking video player
Disable uBlock Origin for that site temporarily, or whitelist just the video domain
Login button does nothing
Script blocked by uBlock Origin
Click the uBlock icon → toggle "Disable on this site"
Page loads blank
Overly aggressive protection
Use your backup browser for this site, or temporarily switch protection to "Standard"
Comments or reviews missing
Third-party scripts blocked
Check for a site "privacy mode" setting; some sites detect blockers and hide content
CAPTCHA loop (infinite puzzles)
Privacy settings interfering with CAPTCHA
Enable "Temporary allow all" in uBlock Origin for that session
Payments failing
Payment processor script blocked
Whitelist the payment domain, e.g. *.stripe.com or *.braintree.com

How to Whitelist a Site

  1. Click the uBlock Origin icon in your toolbar.
  2. Click the large power button to disable blocking on the current site only.
  3. Reload the page — the site reactivates its scripts immediately.
  4. If only one feature is broken (like a video or a login), prefer disabling the specific tracker/script instead of the whole page — click the icon, open the dashboard, and unblock the single domain causing the issue.
  5. Whitelisting is per-site and persists until you toggle it back — review your whitelist occasionally and remove sites you no longer trust.
FROM: ADVANCED CONFIGURATIONS

FINGERPRINTING & TOOL ISSUES

PROBLEM
LIKELY CAUSE
SOLUTION
Fingerprint test still says "unique"
Strict/randomization setting not actually enabled
Re-check Enhanced Tracking Protection (Firefox) or Shields (Brave), then retest
Password manager won't autofill
Browser's built-in password manager conflicting with the extension
Disable your browser's native password manager per the Password Managers setup step
DNS filtering blocks a site I need
Overly aggressive DNS-level blocklist
Whitelist the domain in your NextDNS/ControlD/AdGuard DNS dashboard
FROM: TEST & VERIFY

TEST SUITE ISSUES

PROBLEM
LIKELY CAUSE
SOLUTION
"GPC signal detected" doesn't show
Browser doesn't support GPC, or it's off
Safari and stock Chrome don't support it natively; switch to Brave, Firefox, or LibreWolf
DNS leak test shows my ISP
VPN or private DNS not actually active during the test
Confirm your VPN/DNS provider is connected, then rerun the test
Ad-blocker test shows slots as "not blocked"
Extension disabled on that domain, or filter list out of date
Re-enable on the domain and update filter lists from the extension's dashboard
