A Practical Guide to Browser Privacy

The step-by-step guide that actually works — four short parts, no jargon, five minutes to get meaningfully protected.

QUICK START

Get protected in about 5 minutes. Click a step to see the details.

Pick Brave, Mullvad Browser, or LibreWolf and set it as your default. See Part 1: Choose Your Browser for the full picture, including a two- or three-browser lineup.

Install uBlock Origin — verify the developer is Raymond Hill (gorhill). It's the single most effective privacy tool you can add. Full walkthrough in Part 2: Set Up Your Browser.

Firefox/LibreWolf: Enhanced Tracking Protection → Strict. Brave: Shields set to Aggressive. This also cuts down fingerprinting — see Part 3: Advanced Tools for browser-specific detail.

Visit coveryourtracks.eff.org and confirm strong tracking protection. Full test suite in Part 4: Test & Verify.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Every request your browser makes hands trackers your IP, device details, and behavior — building a profile that follows you across the web. Cookies are only half the story: fingerprinting can identify you even after you clear them. The good news is that a handful of deliberate choices — the right browser, one extension, one setting — close most of that gap in a few minutes.

Visit a site →
Trackers read your IP, device, fonts, timezone →
A profile of you is built and follows you site to site →
The right browser + settings break this chain.
