How Secure Is My Password?

Estimates how long a password would take to crack based on length and character variety. Type a similar-but-fake password, not your real one.

Have I Been Pwned

Checks your email address (not your password) against known data breaches, so you know which accounts need a fresh password now.

The safest way to "check" a password is to never type your real one into a website at all. Rely on your password manager's built-in strength meter and breach-monitoring feature instead.

STRONG PASSWORD TIPS

1

Length beats complexity — a long passphrase is harder to crack than a short jumble of symbols.

2

Never reuse a password across two different accounts.

3

Let your password manager generate it — random beats anything memorable.

4

Add 2FA with an authenticator app — a strong password alone can still be phished.

PASSWORD MANAGERS

A password manager generates and remembers a unique password per site, so a breach at one service can't unlock the rest of your accounts.

Bitwarden KeePassXC Proton Pass 1Password Intelius

Bitwarden is a solid free starting point. Install the extension, disable your browser's built-in password manager, and start generating unique passwords — beginning with your email and banking accounts.
