These are copy-paste starting points, not legal advice — fill in the brackets, then paste the text into a contact form or email. Swap out anything that doesn't apply to your situation.

TEMPLATE 1: DATA BROKER REMOVAL

Copy and paste this into contact forms or emails.

Tip: Many data brokers have a dedicated opt-out form. Check their footer for "Do Not Sell My Info" or "Privacy Request" links first.

TEMPLATE 2: GDPR/CCPA REMOVAL REQUEST

Use this if the company is based in the EU (GDPR) or California (CCPA).

Note: This works best for companies based in the EU or California. Many US-based companies will ignore GDPR requests.

TEMPLATE 3: GOOGLE RESULTS ABOUT YOU REMOVAL

Google will automatically handle the request through their tool, but you may need to manually submit for certain results.

Request URL: myactivity.google.com/results-about-you

Steps:

  1. Click "Get started"
  2. Add the personal information you want to monitor
  3. When Google finds a result, click "Request removal"
  4. Google will review and remove if it "meets policy requirements"

Google's policy: They will remove results that are "doxxing" or "unwanted" but may not remove public records or news articles.
